# Case Study: Kaizii Microfinance Bank

## Organisation and Scenario

Kaizii Microfinance Bank (Kaizii MFB) is a fictional Nigerian financial service that provides digital banking services to individuals and small businesses. The platform focuses on the needs of African customers, with the possibility of expanding to other African countries in the future.

## Nature of the System

The system allows customers to manage their money, transfer funds, pay bills, and view their transaction history through a digital platform.

## Users of the Database

- Customers: manage accounts, transfer money, and view transactions.
- Customer support staff: help customers with account issues and failed transactions.
- Compliance and audit officers: check customer verification records and review suspicious transactions.
- System services: process transactions and generate reports.

## Major Data Stored

- Customer details and verification records.
- Customer accounts and balances.
- Transactions and financial records.
- Saved beneficiaries and recipients.
- Linked bank account references.
- Audit logs and suspicious transaction alerts.

## Key Operations

1. Register customers and record their verification status.
2. Create and manage customer accounts.
3. Record deposits and withdrawals.
4. Transfer money between accounts.
5. Record failed transactions and process reversals.
6. Generate account statements and transaction reports.
7. Check account balances and review suspicious transactions.

## Database Technology

PostgreSQL will be used to store and manage customer information, accounts, and financial transactions. The first version will focus on Nigeria while considering possible expansion to other African markets.

All customer details and transactions used in this project will be fictional.
