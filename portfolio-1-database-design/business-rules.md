# Kaizii MFB Business Rules

1. Each customer must have a unique customer ID.
2. Each customer must have a unique email address and phone number.
3. A customer can be an individual or a merchant.
4. A customer can have more than one account.
5. Each account belongs to one customer.
6. Each account must have a unique account number.
7. Account balances cannot be negative.
8. Each transaction must have a unique transaction ID and a positive amount.
9. A transaction can involve one or more accounts.
10. Each ledger entry must be linked to an existing transaction and account.
11. A transaction reversal must refer to the original transaction.
12. Customer verification records must be linked to an existing customer.
13. Suspicious transactions can be flagged for review.
14. Financial transaction records should not be deleted when a reversal occurs.
